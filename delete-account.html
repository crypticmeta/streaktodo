Streak Todo · Data control

Delete your data

Streak Todo stores task content locally. Optional Google sign-in and Mixpanel analytics create a small amount of account-associated and product-usage data.

Delete one task or Trash

Open a task and delete it. It moves to Insights → Recently deleted, where you can restore it, remove it permanently, or empty Trash.

Delete everything on the device

Open Insights → Data → Reset local data. This removes local tasks, notes, subtasks, categories, reminders, repeat rules, completion history, and Trash. Default categories are recreated.

Disconnect Google and stop analytics

Use Insights → Account → Sign out to disconnect the app session. Use Insights → Privacy → Share product usage to stop new Mixpanel events. These actions do not by themselves erase analytics already received or delete your Google account.

Request remote-data deletion

Email ankitpathakofficial@gmail.com with the subject “Streak Todo data deletion request.” Include the email address used for Google sign-in so the Mixpanel profile and associated analytics records can be matched. We may ask you to verify the request before processing it.

Google Account and exported backups

CODERIXX cannot delete your Google account. You can separately remove Streak Todo access in your Google Account settings. JSON backups remain wherever you saved or sent them and must be deleted from those destinations separately. Uninstalling removes local app data subject to Android backup and restore settings.
