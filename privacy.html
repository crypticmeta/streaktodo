✦ Streak Todo

Privacy, without the fog.

Effective August 11, 2026. Streak Todo is a local-first task planner published by CODERIXX.

Your task content stays local

Tasks, notes, subtasks, category names, dates, reminder text, repeat rules, completion history, and preferences stay in the app sandbox on your device. Streak Todo has no cloud task sync and does not upload that content to CODERIXX, Google, or Mixpanel.

Optional Google sign-in

You can connect Google or continue locally. If you sign in, Google provides a Google account identifier, email address, display name, and available basic profile fields. The app uses the identifier, email, and name to show the connected account and identify the signed-in user in Mixpanel. It does not store your Google password, request offline access, or store an ID token in its own preferences.

Privacy-bounded Mixpanel analytics

Analytics is on by default. Turn it off any time under Insights → Privacy → Share product usage.

The app sends explicit product events such as app opens, onboarding, successful task/category actions, reminder scheduling, theme changes, and backup/reset actions. Coarse properties can include whether a task has a date, time, notes, category, reminder, repeat rule, a bounded subtask count, or the backup file size.

Never sent to Mixpanel: task titles, notes, subtask text, category names, reminder text, search terms, or backup contents. Automatic event capture and session replay are off. Mixpanel may process standard app/device context, an SDK-generated identifier, and the IP address used for the request. IP geolocation enrichment is disabled, and the EU ingestion endpoint is configured. Signed-in profiles include Google ID, email, and display name.

Backups and links

Export creates a JSON file and opens your system share sheet. Import reads only the file you select. The app does not upload either file. Copies you save or send are controlled by you and the chosen destination. External links open in your browser.

Providers, security, and permissions

Google provides optional authentication and Mixpanel provides product analytics. Data is not sold or used for third-party advertising. App-managed network traffic uses HTTPS. Android's INTERNET permission supports those services; notification, exact-alarm, boot, wake-lock, and vibration permissions support local reminders. The system document picker avoids broad storage permission.

Retention and deletion

  • Delete tasks and Trash items, or use Insights → Data → Reset local data.
  • Sign out to disconnect Google from the app; this does not delete your Google account or previously received analytics.
  • Turn off product usage analytics to stop new Mixpanel events.
  • Use the deletion page or email ankitpathakofficial@gmail.com to request deletion of the Mixpanel profile and associated records. Include the Google sign-in email so the request can be matched; verification may be required.

Exported backups remain at their destination until you delete them. Android device backup can preserve eligible app data according to device settings.

Children and changes

Streak Todo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Material policy changes will update the date above.
